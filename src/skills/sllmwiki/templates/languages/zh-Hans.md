## Writing

- **Register:** plain written Chinese for an engineer (书面语), short sentences, no slang and no filler such as 值得注意的是 or 众所周知.
- **Terms:** on first use, the Chinese term with the English in full-width brackets, 一致性哈希（consistent hashing）, then the Chinese term alone, the same one every time. A term with no settled Chinese translation stays English. Code, commands, file names and API names stay as written.
- **Punctuation:** full-width in Chinese text, ，。：；？！（）, with “” for quotation marks and 《》 for the title of a paper or book; half-width in code, formulas and numbers.
- **Spacing:** a space between Chinese and a Latin word or a number (使用 3 个副本，每个 key 一个), none next to full-width punctuation.
- **Citations and markers:** inside the sentence, before its full stop, after a space: 键只会移动到相邻节点 [3]。 A marker the same: 这是我们的推断 [→]。
- **Quotations:** a quotation from a source in another language keeps its words, in “”, with its translation after it in full-width brackets: “each node is assigned a random position”（每个节点被分配一个随机位置）[2]。
- **Numbers:** Arabic numerals, with the source's units.
